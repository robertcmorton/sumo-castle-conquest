# Sumo Castle Conquest

A browser game designed by an 8-year-old named Leo and built with Claude.

## Play

Open [`sumo-castle-conquest.html`](sumo-castle-conquest.html) in any modern browser.

## How to play

- Click **your tower** then click another tower to send a wave of sumos
- When a tower's HP hits **0** it becomes yours
- Capture the **Red Castle** to win
- Don't let your **Blue Castle** fall

100 levels of difficulty, from baby-easy to brutal. Progress saves automatically.
